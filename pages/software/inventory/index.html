intuitive surgical inventory search

#ui/ux #html #css #javascript

Intuitive Surgical, the maker of da Vinci robotic-assisted surgical systems, worked with Senitron on an RFID deployment at an Intuitive facility in San Jose. Senitron says the project covered a roughly 200,000-square-foot warehouse and installed hundreds of RFID antennas, alongside custom software for real-time inventory and asset tracking.

The system was intended to give staff hands-free, more detailed visibility into items moving through the warehouse and support inventory management. Available sources describe the deployment and its tracking capabilities, but don’t provide a clear installation date, measured business results, or detailed information about the tags themselves.

After installing hundreds of RFID tracking antennas at one of Intuitive's San Jose warehouses, a back-end developer and myself implemented a custom software application to track Intuitive's valuable inventory. I also designed and developed the user interface and experiences for the Search Inventory application.

american apparel melrose inventory

#ui/ux #html #css #javascript

Like most retail locations, American Apparel stores traditionally use a manual item count system - which takes roughly 240 man-hours per month to keep track of per store and leaves a lot of potential for human error.

American Apparel partnered with Senitron to introduce a storewide RFID inventory-tracking system. Senitron supplied the software and installed fixed Impinj readers and antennas, enabling staff to track merchandise by store zone and check inventory without manually counting items. After installing custom-built software and hardware to track RFID tags employees are now able to monitor inventory in real-time, gaining near-complete inventory visibility. Reports attributed to the rollout said accuracy exceeded 99%, shrink fell by 50–75%, and sales increased.
